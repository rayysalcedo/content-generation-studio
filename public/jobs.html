Thumbnail attach jobs.

When you push a course, a thumbnail-attach job is queued here. Your CC360 Custom JS picks up the job automatically the next time you have CC360 open in any tab — typically within 30 seconds. No clicks needed.

How this works: Server can't talk to CC360's internal API directly (their WAF blocks our IP), so we queue the work and let your CC360 browser tab do the actual attach. Make sure CC360 is open and the Custom JS snippet is installed at Agency Settings → Company → Custom JS. If jobs are stuck pending, open app.coursecreator360.com in any tab.
